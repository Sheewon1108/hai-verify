LA County FBN official requirement excerpts:
- Owner must sign.
- Corporate officer if corporation.
- Street address of principal place of business and county must be listed.
- Corporation/limited liability companies must include address as set out in its articles of incorporation on file with the Secretary of State and the state of incorporation/organization.
- Statement must be filed in county of principal place of business.
- Effective January 1, 2024, registrant is required to include a business mailing address instead of a residence address.
- Registered business owners can file completely online; proof of identity, signature, and payment can be completed online; online filing includes a $10.75 service fee.
- All mail requests must include a completed Fictitious Business Name Statement and a notarized Affidavit of Identity form.
